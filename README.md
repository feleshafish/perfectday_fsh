# perfectday_fsh

"My Perfect Day" is a visual novel where you can design your own day, choose what you'd like to do, and reflect on what you took from it. Through this game, you meet your new companion, Leafy! He accompanies you on your "Perfect Day" life-simulation while you journal what your day entailed, with a wide selection of pathways to continue your journey.

This game was created for Hack Club Athena's Sunbeam 2026 @ NYC by Felesha Fei, Sophia Huang, and Arisha Islam. Made with sweat, tears, and don't forget love! :D
