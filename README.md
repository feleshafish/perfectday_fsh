# perfectday_fsh

In "My Perfect Day," you can look forward to designing your own day, the things you'd like to do, and what you've taken from your day by playing this visual novel. Through this game, you meet your new companion, Leafy! He accompanies you on your "Perfect Day" while you can journal what your day entailed, with a large selection of pathways to further your journey.

This game was created for Hack Club Athena's Sunbeam 2026 @ NYC by Felesha Fei, Sophia Huang, and Arisha Islam. Made with love!
